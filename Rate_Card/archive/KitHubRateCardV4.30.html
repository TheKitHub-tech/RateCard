EXTERNAL HIRE RATE CARD

Browse indicative equipment hire prices, add equipment to your basket, and create an Excel booking request for an official quote.

How to use this rate card

  1. Choose your collection and return dates and preferred times, or enter a set number of chargeable days. Weekend equipment use is automatically adjusted to Friday collection and Monday return.
  2. Search or filter the rate card, then add items to your basket.
  3. Enter the client and production contact details, then download the Excel booking request and start an email to the Kit Hub.

Sending your request: the final button downloads the Excel booking request and opens a draft email to the Kit Hub. For browser security, you will still need to attach the downloaded file manually.

Would you like equipment or rooms?

Submitting a request does not confirm a booking. All equipment and room requests are subject to availability, staffing, technical approval and written confirmation from the Kit Hub. Prices are exclusive of VAT and indicative only.

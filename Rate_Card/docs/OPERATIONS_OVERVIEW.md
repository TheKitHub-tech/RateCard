# Operations overview

## What the service offers

The public tool helps external clients and eligible users build an indicative equipment and room request before the Kit Hub prepares an official quote.

The customer can:

1. select Equipment or Rooms & Spaces;
2. choose a shared booking window;
3. search, filter and expand categories;
4. add individual items, rooms or equipment packages;
5. review the Basket;
6. add production/client details;
7. export a Booking Request;
8. start an email and manually attach the exported workbook.

## What staff receive

The Booking Request can contain:

- client and production details;
- equipment collection and return dates/times;
- equipment quantities and indicative prices;
- room dates, times and charging basis;
- mandatory technical support;
- optional services;
- estimated VAT and totals.

Staff still review availability, staffing, technical requirements, eligibility, insurance and commercial terms before issuing an official quote.

## Packages

Packages are ease-of-use presets, not discounted products. Selecting one adds its individual equipment components to the basket at their normal current rates.

Current presets:

- Documentary Interview Kit
- Two-Camera Interview Kit
- Portrait Photography Kit
- Location Sound Kit
- Location Podcast Kit

Packages should also be mirrored in SISO if they become part of the operational booking workflow.

## Privacy

The public page has no account or central database. The active basket and shared dates may remain in the current browser session. Generated files remain on the user's device.

On a shared computer, clear the basket and close the browser after use.

## Management decisions still pending

Terms and conditions, eligibility, quote lead time, minimum notice, insurance/excess, deposits, minimum spend, payment terms and commercial terms require approved wording before publication.

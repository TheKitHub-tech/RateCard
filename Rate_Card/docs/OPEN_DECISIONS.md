# Open management and policy decisions

These items are intentionally not finalised in V4.30. Do not invent public wording.

| Topic | Information required | Current status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Audience and eligibility | Who can hire: alumni, external clients, partner organisations or other groups | Awaiting agreement |
| Terms and conditions | Approved external T&Cs file or stable link, document owner and review date | External T&Cs to be obtained from management |
| Quote lead time | Clear expected response time and wording for repeat follow-ups | Required before wider promotion |
| Minimum booking notice | Minimum working days required before collection or room use | Required |
| Insurance | Whether University cover applies to external hire and the exact client responsibilities | Policy review required |
| Insurance excess | Confirm amount and approved customer-facing wording; £5,000 was an initial belief, not an approved published fact | Not confirmed |
| Deposits | Whether deposits are required and whether a compliant payment route exists | No confirmed card-deposit capability |
| Minimum spend | Whether a minimum booking value will exist | Not agreed and may be omitted |
| Payment terms | Due date, invoicing route, VAT treatment and late-payment wording | Awaiting finance/commercial approval |
| Commercial terms | Cancellations, damage, loss, substitution, collection/return responsibilities and other conditions | Awaiting approved T&Cs |
| Ownership | Named owners for GitHub, Netlify, domain, releases, catalogue data and closure dates | Needs formal assignment |

## Publishing rule

Only add these topics to the public site when approved wording and an accountable owner are available. Add the T&Cs as a maintained link rather than copying a document into the HTML unless the approved publishing process says otherwise.

## Suggested decision record

For each agreed item, record:

- approved wording;
- approving person/team;
- approval date;
- source document or link;
- review date;
- site version that introduced it.
